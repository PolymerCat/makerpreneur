Student Study Plan & Class Schedule
1. I want to make a section for student's daily academic plans and schedule
2. It must be integrated with Google Calendar for easier extension and ecosystem
3. Students can create new events as study sessions or any kind of task they wanna do



Use Cases
1. Students can view calendar for all events or class schedules
2. Students can view the events and class schedules in their google calendar too
3. Students can create class times for their weekly classes and view in calendar
4. Students can create events (aka study sessions etc.) and view on calendar
5. Students can get reminder of their events and classes in the app
